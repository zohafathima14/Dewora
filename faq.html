Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers about how to browse products, use our cart and wishlist, understand our simulated checkout, and learn about our frontend demonstration policies.

Shopping & Product Discovery

You can explore our complete skincare collection by clicking Shop All in the navigation bar. You can also filter directly by category, such as Sunscreen, Moisturizers, Cleansers, Serums, and Body Care, from the primary navigation links or homepage category cards.

Every product card provides the product's image, category badge, name, size, customer rating, price in INR, and quick action buttons for adding to your shopping bag or saving to your wishlist.

You can search for products using the search icon in the header or the keyword input on the Shop page. Search queries match against product titles, categories, short descriptions, and ingredients (such as Niacinamide or Ceramides).

On the catalogue page, you can refine your view using four filter groups: Category, Skin Type (Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive, Normal), Skin Concern (Hydration, Barrier Care, Sun Care, Dullness), and Price ranges (Under ₹300, ₹300–₹500, ₹500–₹700, ₹700–₹1,000, Above ₹1,000). Multiple filters combine using AND logic across groups so you can find exact matches for your routine.

Click the "Add to Bag" button located on any product card or on the individual product detail page. The item is instantly added to your bag, a confirmation toast notification will appear, and the bag badge in the header will update in real time to reflect your current item count.

Click the heart icon on any product card or product detail page to save a product to your wishlist. Clicking the heart again removes the product. You can view all saved items by visiting your Wishlist from the heart icon in the navigation bar.

On the wishlist page, you can remove individual items, move specific products into your shopping bag, or move all saved items into your bag with a single click.

Navigate to your Shopping Bag page. For each item in your bag, accessible + and – stepper buttons allow you to increase or decrease the quantity, or you can enter any positive whole number directly into the quantity field. Totals recalculate immediately.

To remove an item completely, click the "Remove" button or trash icon next to the product line. Your subtotal, delivery status, and header count will adjust automatically.

Delivery & Shipping Thresholds

Complimentary delivery applies when your shopping bag subtotal reaches ₹499 or more. A progress banner on the bag page tracks your current subtotal and displays how close you are to unlocking free delivery.

When your bag reaches ₹499 or more, the order summary highlights a green FREE delivery badge, and no shipping fee is applied.

In this demonstration store, delivery charges for cart subtotals below ₹499 have not been configured. The order summary displays the remaining amount needed to reach ₹499 and transparently notes that delivery charges below ₹499 have not been configured in this demo.

No arbitrary or invented fee is added; your total to pay strictly equals your bag subtotal.

Demonstration Storefront, Security & Privacy

Proceeding to Checkout presents a 3-step progress flow (Bag > Delivery Details > Confirmation). You can enter sample recipient information, phone number, and address details.

When you click "Place Demo Order", the system validates required fields and formatting in your browser, disables the button to prevent double-clicks, simulates an 800ms order processing delay, and displays an order confirmation screen with a generated reference number (e.g. DEW-DEMO-XXXXXX).

No. Dewora is a frontend-only demonstration storefront. No real payment gateways or processors are connected. No credit card, debit card, UPI ID, net banking password, or digital wallet credentials are ever requested or accepted.

No. No physical products are manufactured, packed, or dispatched. The entire ordering flow, including order reference numbers and confirmation summaries, is simulated entirely within your browser for demonstration and interface design purposes.

Your shopping bag and wishlist items are saved locally on your device using standard browser storage (localStorage). This ensures that your selections persist seamlessly as you navigate between pages or refresh the browser.

Stored data consists strictly of product IDs and quantities (e.g. [{"productId":"dew-sun-001","quantity":1}]). No remote database or analytics service is used.

No. In accordance with strict privacy principles, all contact and delivery information entered during checkout (such as name, email, phone number, and address) is handled strictly in browser memory for the immediate confirmation view.

Customer information is never saved to localStorage, sessionStorage, or cookies, and is never transmitted over any network. Once you leave the confirmation view or refresh your browser, that in-memory information is immediately discarded.
